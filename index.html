About me

Designing the bridge between learning, people and technology.

I’m a Learning Technologies designer working across instructional design, UX/UI and interactive development. I begin by understanding the people, the context and the real learning need—then translate that understanding into a clear structure, an intuitive interface and an experience people can actively use.

Because I work across disciplines, I can carry an idea from early research and experience strategy through prototyping and implementation, while keeping the learning goal and the user’s experience connected throughout the process.
